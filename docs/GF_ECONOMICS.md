# GF ECONOMICS: Fungible Unit & Supply Mechanics

## 1. Role of GF (GeneticFrames Unit)
`GF` is the native fungible utility unit of the GeneticFrames ecosystem. 
It functions as:
1. **Generation Unit**: 1 GF = 1 generation event (`GENERATE`).
2. **Medium of Exchange**: Settlement asset for peer-to-peer bids and asks on the marketplace.
3. **Unit of Account**: Standard denomination for asset valuation and market indexes.

---

## 2. Generation Transformation (Burn Mechanism)

The core economic transformation is:

$$\text{1 GF (Fungible)} \xrightarrow{\text{Burn} + \text{Computation}} \text{1 GeneticFrame (Non-Fungible)}$$

When an agent triggers generation:
1. 1 GF is burned (or committed to protocol reserve).
2. Compute resources acquire and canonicalize genomic data.
3. A unique non-fungible asset enters circulation.

```
Total Circulating GeneticFrames = Total Historical Generation Events
```

---

## 3. Marketplace Protocol Fees

To ensure sustainable infrastructure operations without speculative distortion:
* **Generation Fee**: 0% additional protocol surcharge (pure 1 GF burn).
* **Secondary Market Trades**: 1.5% protocol fee deducted from the seller in GF, deposited to protocol treasury or burned.
